## 1. Re-derivation

**Tools.**
- *Fact R.* With |Q| ≤ 3 and every good ≤ v_i(y_i), only the strict minimum can fail robustness:
  v(y_i) + v(h) ≥ max + min.
- *Fact K.* Immediate.
- *Tool T* is Lemma 2\* at the picks with owner o.
  - (H1) for frozen q ≠ k: q values no junk, and q does not want B_o, since o is unfrozen.
  - (H1) for k: hypothesis (i).
  - Robust helpers are admissible by Lemma 4.
  - The bounds "goods of W_o ∩ R_i worth ≤ p_i, |W_o ∩ R_i| ≤ 3" follow from (I2), o unfrozen, and y_i ∉ W_o.
- *Lemma O.* Only z or an agent of U with a pick can be crowded: frozen and pickless agents value at most Y_z in Π. A
  crowded holder has at most one good of Q_u outside H₀.
  - (a) is Fact R.
  - (b): Q_z = {y_z, g₁, g₂}, and v_z(a) + v_z(y_z) > v_z(g₁) + v_z(g₂).
  - Distinct options give an SDR for Lemma D1-red 4, with no HEAD.
  - All correct.

**Lemma C3.** Owner o ∈ U, H = {g}, helper z.
- W_o ∩ R_z ⊆ R_z ∖ {a, y_z} has ≤ 2 goods, so g is robust.
- R_k ⊇ {Y_k, y_z, g} leaves (W_o ∖ {g}) ∩ R_k at most one good, which is not improving.

Correct.

**Lemma HC.**
- *First case:* H₀ = Q_z = P_k = {y_z, g, e}. Only k and z value junk, and every good is relevant to someone, so
  Π = P_k and Π does not threaten k. This is the only use of "no null goods".
- *Second case:* the allocation A (x takes {a} ∪ (J ∖ g), z takes {y_z, g}) is EFX₀. All four views were re-derived:
  - z's pair, by (U) and (I2);
  - x's bundle seen by z, by crowding and "no option";
  - x's bundle seen by k: at most one good, with v_k(a) ≤ p_k by (π);
  - x's bundle seen by any other agent: a has no second wanter, since z is a holder at B′.

  Since A is a one-absorber completion of the picks, hardness forbids it. Correct.

**Corollary U0** is then immediate.

**§3.3.** Each case was re-derived; the shapes are exhaustive given no improving single good and |P_k| ≤ 3.
- (F1)–(F3) are the only ways distinct options can fail.
- A-a–c: a single member of M, so Tool T(z, {h}).
- A-d: b is non-robust everywhere, so it is the strict minimum. Two distinct helpers would give T(z, {c, d}). So
  A_{bc} = A_{bd} = {u} with option d.
- A-e: |𝒜| = 1 would force three strict minima. Otherwise every R(u) is one singleton {h}, contradicting
  A_{P_k ∖ {h}} ≠ ∅.
- B with |T_k| = 1: U = {o}. Either Tool T(z, {c}), or the option e.
- B with |T_k| = 2: R_k is full and {b, c} ∈ M.
  - M = {{b,c}}: T(z, {h}).
  - Two pairs: (F2) gives T(u₁, ·) by the three sub-cases of W_{u₁} ∩ R_{u₂}; (F3) gives T(u, {b}).
  - Three pairs: U = {u}, and both leftovers would be strict minima.

Correct.
